# Writer brief — Copper Kettle Valley quest text

Fifteen rules. They are binding on every quest description, NPC line, toast, noticeboard string and journal page. Sources: `docs/story-research.md`, `docs/story-gap-analysis.md`, `story/story-final.md` §3, §8, §10.

1. **Every quest description has three parts, in this order: one voice line (≤25 words, spoken by a named resident or Josie), the literal instruction, one line of what it unlocks or who it helps.** Never a lore quest and a how-to quest as separate quests — the voice and the task live in the same object.

2. **The instruction names the item, the count and the place.** Never "build a base," "make it nice," "gather some," "when you're ready." If the player must judge anything, the quest is wrong.

3. **Restate the destination in the same words, every time: "Forty lamps. Fifteen people. One winter that nobody leaves."** It appears on the first-join screen, in Q1, in every act header, on the noticeboard, and at Q90. Never paraphrase it.

4. **Never write a timer, a deadline, a fail state or a "before it's too late."** The calendar is pressure; it is never a threat. No "in one session," no "hurry," no "you'll lose."

5. **Every quest names the next thing in plain words in its reward toast** — the pack cannot pin a quest, so the text is the pin. "Next: Marnie needs three eggs."

6. **Reference material ships one quest before the quest that needs it.** Tuning numbers, readouts, maps, recipes, compasses, waystones, tools. If a quest is a puzzle, its notes were the previous quest's reward. Check this before submitting any quest that says "a problem, not a recipe."

7. **Any "go find" quest names the place in the text, ships the compass already set to it, and a waystone is already standing there.** There are exactly four named destinations in the pack; nothing else is ever open-ended.

8. **Any decorate quest lists every block, ships every block, and every spot is marked. End it with "Any order is right."** She gets the arranging; she never gets the empty lot.

9. **Name the gate item and who has it.** "Bram can't build the wheel until the boards are dry — that's the oven, and that's you." Gates are ingredients visible in EMI; the text says so in plain words so nobody opens a wiki.

10. **Residents talk in their own register, two sentences maximum.** Marnie warm and busy · Bram short and technical · Oda precise, counts things · Nella flat and dry · Halden slow, indirect · Tobin fast, over-explains · Wisp cheerful with slightly wrong grammar · Pip loud, names everything. Josie is wry, practical, never sad for long, and never present.

11. **Arcs close on screen with a line, never a stat.** No "friendship increased," no "reputation gained," no progress bars for people. Bram saying "well" is the whole payoff.

12. **Write payoffs that render.** A lit lamp, a plushie, a named dish, a person standing where they weren't, a room that is warm. Never "you feel proud," "the town is grateful," "a sense of accomplishment."

13. **Every act finale's last line must work as the pack's last line.** If development stops at any finale, that finale is the ending. Write each one as though it might be.

14. **Never assume a wiki, a Discord, a second monitor or a partner who already knows.** If the player needs a number, the number is in the quest text or the journal — not in JEI, not in a mod's own docs.

15. **Cut any sentence that is only atmosphere.** Quest text competes with the thing the player is trying to do. Atmosphere belongs in the journal, on the cellar wall, and in the five found books. Three lines per quest is the ceiling, not the target.
